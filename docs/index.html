Issun-bōshi, the one-inch boy of the tale, goes up the river in his bowl: he dodges logs, skewers fish, birds and whatever comes out of the bank, picks up what they leave and, once ashore, takes on the oni of each scene. Five scenes and an ending. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the eight tunes, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1987 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump 02a2fafc...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, the river and what comes down it, the bars, the falls and GAME OVER.
  • The scenes: the five scenes: the river, the bank, the oni on land and the ending.
  • The sprites: Issun-boushi, his bowl, every enemy and the end put together from the ROM tables, frame by frame.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, where it shows and its translation.
  • The music: the eight tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: TAB and CAPS on the title, a demo that plays the cartridge header, shared bytes, leftovers.
  • Secrets and bugs: what was searched for: TAB and CAPS, the demo that plays the cartridge, the high score set to zero past 9,999,990, the cursor keys and joystick mixed, and the life at 10,000 checked.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the loop, the river map, the sprites, the RAM and the tools.
